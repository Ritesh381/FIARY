# 🔥 FIARY — Your Integrated Life Dashboard

**FIARY** (a friendly wordplay on *Diary* with *feedback*) is a personal life dashboard that unifies journaling, habits, productivity, finance, and AI-driven insights — all in one elegant interface.  
It’s designed to help you stop guessing and start understanding how your daily actions impact your well-being and productivity.

---

## 🚀 Motivation & Vision

Managing your personal life shouldn’t require juggling five different apps.

**FIARY** was built from a simple frustration:  
> *“Why can’t one platform intelligently connect how I feel, work, and spend my time?”*

### **Core Idea**
To provide **one integrated dashboard** where journaling, habits, tasks, and finances interconnect — enabling pattern discovery, reflection, and smarter daily decisions.

### **Vision**
FIARY isn’t just a diary — it’s your **mirror**, **manager**, and **motivator**, helping you:
- 🕰 **Understand Time:** Visualize where your time goes.
- 🔁 **Stay Consistent:** Maintain habits and track streaks.
- 💭 **Reflect & Improve:** Learn from AI-generated insights.
- 💸 **Stay Aware:** Track your financial health and behavior.

## 🧩 Core Features

| **Feature** | **Description** | **Status** |
| :----------- | :-------------- | :---------- |
| **Journaling** | Daily log of mood, sleep, achievements, time wasted, and notes. | ✅ Complete |
| **Authentication** | Secure user authentication and session handling. | ✅ Complete |
| **Habit Tracker** | Track “Develop” or “Quit” habits with progress analytics. | ✅ Complete |
| **Tasks & Todos** | Manage one-time todos and repeating task templates. | ✅ Complete |
| **Finance Tracker** | Log income and expenses, categorize transactions, and view monthly totals. | ✅ Complete |
| **AI Insights (Weekly / Monthly)** | Auto-generated summaries analyzing your logs, habits, and trends. | ✅ Complete |
| **Memories (Moments)** | Save and relive meaningful life events. | ✅ Complete |
| **Thoughts Vault** | Quick idea capture for journaling or creative notes. | ✅ Complete |
| **Profile Page** | User profile editing (avatar, bio, personalization). | 🧠 In Progress |
| **The Shelf** | Manage media collections: books, movies, games, and courses. | 🧠 In Progress |

---

## 🧠 The Final Boss: *The Mirror*

> “Why did my mood drop last week, and was it related to my sleep or spending?”

**The Mirror** is FIARY’s ultimate AI companion — a personalized chatbot powered by your own data (journals, habits, transactions, etc.).

It provides **context-aware insights**, **data-backed reflections**, and **actionable suggestions**, enabling true self-awareness powered by AI.
